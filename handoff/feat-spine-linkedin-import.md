Every row: fixed id, `metadata.source`, `metadata.date_precision` (LinkedIn dates are months; day = `01`), a comment naming its export row and its `REVIEW` items. Entries and links upsert on id with a `where … is distinct from` guard (a no-op apply touches no `updated_at`); relations are written by slug via scalar subselects, so a missing slug is a `23502` and the seed fails loudly. Bodies are dollar-quoted markdown, editable without escaping. Scanned: no email, phone, address, connection, or prohibited term.
