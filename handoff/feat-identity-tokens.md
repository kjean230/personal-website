## Shipped
- `design/tokens/tokens.css` — the design tokens for identity C · Ink & paper, as CSS custom properties and the **source of truth**: palette (the column C hexes reproduced exactly), type scale (Inter / JetBrains Mono families with system fallbacks, 1.25 ratio, leading, weights, tracking), 4 px spacing scale, sizes (44 px touch minimum, icon sizes, 3 px icon stroke, 2 px accent outline), radii (2 / 3 / 4 px, pill, circle), motion (Glide `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`, 180 / 260 / 320 ms, 1200 ms boot), focus ring, borders, and hard-offset elevation. Light in `:root`, dark under `:root[data-theme="dark"]` and `@media (prefers-color-scheme: dark)`, every duration 0 ms under `prefers-reduced-motion`. C's rule — on paper every accent fill carries a 2 px ink outline; on ink saffron stands alone — is carried as tokens: `--color-outline-accent`, `--border-accent`, `--color-focus-ring`, `--color-mark`, `--color-shadow` flip per theme. `--color-primary-text` (`#7A5A00`) is the light-theme text variant of saffron.
- `design/tokens/build.mjs` — zero-dependency Node generator and linter (Node 18+). Parses `tokens.css`, asserts the two dark blocks are identical and every `var()` resolves, computes WCAG 2.2 AA contrast for every token pair in both themes, lints every SVG against the construction rules and the no-hardcoded-colour rule, sweeps for prohibited terms, and emits the three files below. `--check` exits non-zero on stale output, a failing pair, or a rule break — ready to become a CI step in S1.
- `design/tokens/tokens.json` — generated JSON mirror (static, light, dark, and fully resolved values).
- `design/tokens/CONTRAST.md` — generated contrast table, one row per token pair: 68 required measurements (22 text pairs at 4.5:1 and 12 non-text pairs at 3:1, × 2 themes), **all pass**. Two rule-bound rows (bare saffron on paper, 1.74:1 / 1.92:1) are footnoted exactly as 0a did — the measured boundary is the 2 px ink outline at 16.10:1 — and one informative row covers the decorative hairline divider. Ratios match the 0a page (16.10, 9.27, 5.67, 9.26).
- `design/tokens/specimen.html` — generated review sheet consuming only `var()`s: mark, swatches, surfaces/borders/focus/elevation, type, spacing, radii, motion demo, trophy states, all icons at 24 and 36 px, and an in-context strip — both themes side by side, theme scopes generated from the parsed source, no external requests.
- `design/assets/mark/kj-mark.svg` (bare monogram, `currentColor`, exact 0a geometry) and `kj-badge.svg` (saffron tile with the mark; `var()` colours with light-value fallbacks, so it renders the paper variant standalone and follows the theme inline).
- `design/assets/icons/` — 29 icons hand-drawn on a 24 px grid, 3 px outlines, square caps, mitred joins, `currentColor`, each with a `<title>`: `boot`, `profile-select`, `tile-experience`, `tile-projects`, `tile-certifications`, `tile-education`, `tile-hobbies`, `tile-news`, `all-software`, `groups`, `detail`, `trophy-locked`, `trophy-in-progress`, `trophy-unlocked`, `play-activity`, `album`, `controllers`, `settings`, `sleep`, `notifications`, `hint-a`, `hint-b`, `facet-all`, `facet-corporate`, `facet-research`, `facet-volunteer`, `facet-classroom`, `facet-coursework`, `reaction-toast`. `trophy-unlocked` is the only fill (`var(--color-primary, #E8B11B)` inside the outline). `tile-news` also serves the News rail item in brief §5.

## Deviated from plan
- Two owner decisions that the session prompt left as `[FILL IN]` were taken in-session: **chime composed by the agent** (delivered as code, per the brief's no-binaries rule and the absence of Storage before S2) and **typefaces Inter + JetBrains Mono** (both OFL, loaded by the app at build time, never committed). Both are recorded in `DESIGN.md`.
- The generator also emits `specimen.html` and lints the SVGs. Neither is a plan-row deliverable; both exist so the 0b checklist items — tokens nowhere hardcoded, contrast present and passing, zero third-party assets — are verified mechanically rather than by assertion.
- The generator is Node ESM rather than the throwaway Python used in 0a, so S1 can call it from `package.json` without a second runtime. The chime renderer is a classic script rather than an ES module because module scripts do not load from `file://`; S1 ports it to TypeScript.
- Facet chip glyphs cover the five facets in the data model (`corporate`, `research`, `volunteer`, `classroom`, `coursework`) plus `all`; brief §4.2 names four chips plus All, §4 adds `coursework`. Drawing all six was the assumption.
- `trophy-in-progress` carries a stroke-only level mark inside the cup, so the in-progress state is not distinguished from locked by colour alone; it stays within C's fills-only-for-unlocked rule. The A/B hint glyphs use plain square frames; the 2 px `--radius-xs` applies to real `<kbd>` elements in CSS, not to the icon outlines, which the construction rules keep mitred.
- Phase 0 carve-out as in 0a: PR opened without auto-merge, merged by hand; CI items in §2.1 and §7 recorded as N/A.
