## Deviated from plan
- **The chore branch was created with `--no-track`.** Branching from `origin/lane/spine` the ordinary way sets the upstream to `lane/spine`, which is what makes a bare `git push` refuse — and git then *suggests* `git push origin HEAD:lane/spine`, which writes straight to the protected lane and skips the PR and CI entirely. `--no-track` removes the hazard at the source: a bare `git push` now suggests `--set-upstream origin chore/spine-s8-closeout`, which is the correct command. The cost is that `git status` no longer prints ahead/behind against the lane.
- **Four commits, not three.** The owner's two content corrections (`supabase/seed.sql`, `BUILD_PLAN.md`) are a different kind of change from the close-out record, so they are their own commit rather than being folded into the handoff commit. Order: the `PROMPTS.md` isolation, the `CLAUDE.md` rule, the two corrections, then the annotations and this file.
- **This chore edits two files a close-out would normally not touch**, both on the owner's explicit instruction and both recorded above: `supabase/seed.sql` (comment text only — `CLAUDE.md`'s "a migration that has reached `lane/spine` is never edited" governs *migrations*; seeds are re-runnable idempotent upserts, and this changed no SQL) and `BUILD_PLAN.md` (an authority document; the row was struck and annotated rather than deleted, so its history stays legible).
- **The JS-disabled driver lives in the scratchpad, not the repo.** Prior handoffs record that the driver "must live inside the repo" because Node resolves `node_modules` upward from the script's own directory. That is true for a bare `import`, but it is not the only mechanism: `createRequire("<repo>/package.json")` resolves `puppeteer-core` from outside the repo and was used here. This matters because `tsconfig.json` includes `**/*.ts` / `**/*.mts` with only `node_modules` excluded, and `eslint.config.mjs` ignores only `.next/`, `out/`, `build/`, `next-env.d.ts` and `design/` — so an in-repo driver would enter `typecheck` or `lint`. Keeping it out removes a staging-discipline risk instead of managing it.
- **`db:reset` destroyed the `db-data` volume and the two compose containers that had been up ~22 hours.** That is what a from-clean verification asks for, and it is named here rather than left as a surprise. The containers were left **running** at the end, per the S8 precedent.
- **Two measurement mistakes, both caught and corrected before anything was written down**, recorded because the next close-out will hit them. (1) Counting elements with `grep -o` on the raw served HTML **over-counts**: `data-status` appears twice per certification row, once on the `<li>` and once on the inner `<span>`, so a raw grep reports 10 for 5 rows. The script-stripped DOM measurement is the authoritative one. (2) `document.body.innerText` **does not contain "Unlocked"** on `/certifications`, because the meta line is `text-transform: uppercase` and `innerText` returns rendered text — it reads `UNLOCKED · CERTIFICATION`. Use `textContent` for a case-sensitive label check, and a negative lookbehind when probing for the word `Locked`.
