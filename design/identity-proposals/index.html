Console identity proposals

Phase 0a · three complete, distinct identities for the portfolio's console shell. Each column is a whole identity: the KJ mark, an accent palette with semantic states in light and dark themes, an icon direction with three sample icons drawn from scratch, a motion curve with a named easing and duration range, and a one-line boot chime concept.

Specimens are shown in both themes side by side; nothing on this page toggles. The contrast table at the end of each column reports every text-on-surface pair against WCAG 2.2 AA — 4.5:1 for text, and 3:1 for non-text components as an informative extra. Everything here is original work; see the note at the end of the page.

To pick: reply with A, B, or C.

A · Warm & rounded

Soft geometry, warm neutrals, teal and amber. Friendly and approachable — the shell reads as a companion, not a machine.

Mark

One continuous rounded stroke: the K's lower leg runs straight into the J's hook, so the two letters share a terminal. Pills and quarter-arcs on a 4-unit grid, 10-unit stroke, round terminals. Holds at 24 px in the status bar.

Icon direction

2 px monoline on a 24 px grid, round caps and joins, 3 px inner corner radius. Outlines only; fills appear only to express state.

Motion

Chime

Two ascending marimba notes a soft fifth apart, under half a second, with a warm room tail.

Icon direction

Filled silhouettes on a 24 px grid, corners chamfered at 45°, 1 px optical gaps between parts instead of outlines. Counters are cut, never stroked.

Motion

Chime

C · Ink & paper

Paper and ink neutrals, one saffron accent, slate for structure. Editorial and heavy-lined — the shell reads like a printed programme.

Mark

Heavy slab forms with stencil cuts: the K's arms and the J's hook are separated from their stems by a 2-unit break. Butt terminals, slab serifs top and bottom. On paper the mark is ink; on ink it is saffron.

One accent. Saffron is the active item and the reward; slate does structure, dividers and info. On paper, every accent fill carries a 2 px ink outline, and saffron as running text uses the deepened primary-text; on ink, saffron stands alone. Semantic states are muted earth hues so the single accent stays the loudest thing on screen.

Icon direction

3 px outlines on a 24 px grid, square caps, mitred joins, no rounding. Fills appear only for the active or unlocked item, in saffron, inside the outline.

Motion

Glide — cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1), 180–320 ms. Fast departure, long deceleration; things arrive like a page settling. Reduced motion: the same end state, zero duration.

Chime

A soft low sine thud followed by a single bright click — like a stamp landing on paper.

What is borrowed, and what is not

The shell borrows an interaction grammar — flat, high-contrast surfaces; thick rounded-square tiles in a horizontal, recency-ordered row; a bottom rail of circular utility icons; a top status bar with identity left and clock/status right; corner button hints; near-instant transitions and minimal click-style sound. That grammar is a functional layout convention shared by many console and launcher interfaces. Every mark, icon, palette, easing curve and chime concept on this page is original work: no third-party console marks, logotypes, typefaces, characters, box art, hardware renderings or chimes were used, traced or referenced as assets, and no red-with-blue accent pairing appears in any proposal. The in-context strips above are flat interface layouts, not devices.

Contrast method. Ratios use the WCAG 2.x formula: sRGB channels linearised, relative luminance weighted 0.2126 / 0.7152 / 0.0722, ratio = (Llighter + 0.05) / (Ldarker + 0.05), rounded to two decimals. Thresholds: 4.5:1 for text (1.4.3), 3:1 for non-text components (1.4.11, shown as informative rows). Palette hexes, specimen colours and table values were generated from one source, so they cannot disagree.

What happens after the pick. The chosen identity becomes the input to Phase 0b: design tokens, the full icon set, the mark as an asset, the chime asset, and DESIGN.md. Nothing on this page is a token yet.
